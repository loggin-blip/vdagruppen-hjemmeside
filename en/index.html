We show Massivlust in use · 60 minutes · free

Situation

Massivlust installs mass timber elements for main contractors. The installers work on building sites in several counties at once, and everything that happens out there comes in as messages: what was installed, the truck that came five hours late, the element that was labelled wrong at the factory. The project managers and the owner had to keep track of it all on top of their own jobs.

What we connected

No systems were replaced. We connected to Tripletex, Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, and put one chat in Telegram on top. The installers write to one team chat. The project managers and the owner each have their own assistant in the same app. The hours are mirrored from Tripletex every 15 minutes, so the answer to “how many hours do we have on the project” is never more than a quarter of an hour old.

What happens every day now

The installer writes one message when the day is over. If it says “props didn't arrive” or “no crane before 10:00”, that is stored in the same database as the hours. The project manager writes “need minutes and for you to put them in the folder asap” or “can you make an SJA for the mass timber installation?”, and the assistant sets it up. The owner gets a morning report at 08:30 and an evening report at 17:00, and asks about hours in the same chat.

Figures

Measured in Massivlust's system on 19.10.2026. The hours are mirrored from Tripletex every 15 minutes: 2,872 hours, 11 people, 3 projects. 61 deviations documented between 20.11.2025 and 14.10.2026, at least 32 of them caused by others: factory, transport, design. None of them has a claim registered. That last figure is the reason for the next step.

What comes next

The deviations have text and hours, but no kroner. Only 1 of 51 projects in the system has a budget. What we are building now is profitability per project: how much has been spent against how much is done, so the owner sees it in the same chat every week. It is not live yet, and we say so.
